## Mandatory Study Method for Every Topic

প্রতিটি topic-এ এই 10 প্রশ্নের উত্তর লিখবেন:

```text
1. Business-এ কী ঘটেছে?
2. Financial event কি?
3. কোন accounts affected?
4. Account types কী?
5. কোনটা increase/decrease?
6. Debit/Credit কী হবে?
7. Journal কখন post হবে?
8. কোন subledger update হবে?
9. কীভাবে reversal হবে?
10. কোন report-এ impact পড়বে?
```

Architecture-এর জন্য আরও প্রশ্ন:

```text
11. Source document কোনটা?
12. Accounting event কী?
13. Posting rule configurable নাকি fixed?
14. Account mapping কোথা থেকে resolve হবে?
15. Idempotency কীভাবে হবে?
16. Period lock কীভাবে check হবে?
17. Audit trail কী থাকবে?
18. Reconciliation কীভাবে হবে?
19. Failure হলে rollback কীভাবে হবে?
20. কোন automated tests লাগবে?
```

## 6-Month High-Level Learning Timeline

এই roadmap-এর practical high-level timeline:

```text
Month 1–2
Accounting Foundation + Core Accounting Flows

Month 3
Financial Statements + Inventory + Payroll + Asset Accounting

Month 4
Hospital + School + Budget Accounting

Month 5
Accounting Architecture + Posting Engine + Subledger + Reconciliation

Month 6
Advanced Accounting Architecture + Full Capstone Accounting System
```

Detailed view:

| Month | Main Focus | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Month 1 | Accounting equation, debit/credit, COA, journal | Basic accounting transaction confidently analyze করা |
| Month 2 | AR, AP, cash/bank, revenue, expense, accrual, prepaid | Common business accounting flows understand করা |
| Month 3 | Financial statements, inventory, payroll, asset | Operational modules-এর accounting impact design করা |
| Month 4 | Hospital, school, budget accounting | Domain-specific accounting flows independently model করা |
| Month 5 | Posting engine, events, subledger, GL, reconciliation | Production accounting architecture understand/design করা |
| Month 6 | Reliability, audit, reversal, idempotency, reporting, capstone | End-to-end generic accounting system architect করা |
